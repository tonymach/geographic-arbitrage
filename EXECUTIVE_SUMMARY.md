# Geographic Arbitrage Opportunities - Executive Summary

**Date**: November 2025
**Analysis**: 13 AI agents across 3 phases
**Markets Analyzed**: US, Poland, Romania
**Categories**: Construction Management, Fleet Management

---

## Executive Summary

We identified **8 arbitrage opportunities** where US software companies with $300M-$1.2B valuations have zero or weak presence in Poland/Romania. Top opportunities show 37-360x ROI potential with strong unit economics (5-14x LTV:CAC ratios).

**Market Gap**: US construction software market is 55x larger than Poland ($2.49B vs $35-45M), yet Poland's largest local player is only $10-15M ARR. Similar gaps exist across fleet management.

- LTV:CAC: 14.8x

- Year 1: 50 customers, $1.2M revenue
- Year 3: 300 customers, $7.2M revenue, $2.52M EBITDA

## Risk Factors

**Market Risks**:
1. **Competition**: Geotab, Samsara, AddSecure already in Poland fleet market
2. **Adoption**: Low software adoption baseline in Polish SMB construction
3. **Localization**: Full Polish language/integration required adds cost
4. **Price Sensitivity**: Need 30-50% discount vs US pricing

**Execution Risks**:
1. **CAC**: Could be higher than estimates if sales cycle longer
2. **Retention**: Assumes 3-4 year retention; churn could be higher
3. **Market Size**: TAM estimates based on market reports, not ground truth

**Mitigation**:
- Start with pilot (5-10 customers) to validate economics before full build
- Focus on underserved segments (SMB fleets, residential construction)
- Partner with local players (insurers, industry associations) for distribution
- Price aggressively to gain market share (40-50% below incumbents)
