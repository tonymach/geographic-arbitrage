RESEARCH TASKS:

1. SEARCH GLOBAL PLATFORMS (G2, Capterra)
   - Query: "{category} {region}"
   - Query: "{category} {native_language_term}"
   - Filter results by region: {region}
   - Find top 10-20 products
   - Capture: reviews, ratings, pricing

2. SEARCH LOCAL PLATFORMS
   {platform_list}
   - Search for {category} discussions
   - Find mentioned products
   - Capture adoption signals

3. ANALYZE EACH PRODUCT:
   For each product found:
   - Product name
   - Review count
   - Average rating (out of 5)
   - Estimated pricing
   - Target customer segment
   - Key features
   - Adoption signals (forum mentions, job postings)

4. CALCULATE ADOPTION SCORE:
   adoption_score = (reviews * 0.6) + (rating * 20) + (forum_mentions * 0.3)

   This gives weight to:
   - Review volume (social proof)
   - Quality (rating)
   - Local adoption (forums)
