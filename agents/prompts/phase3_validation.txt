PHASE 3: VALIDATION & UNIT ECONOMICS AGENT

MISSION:
Validate opportunity and calculate PE-grade unit economics

OPPORTUNITY TO VALIDATE:
{opportunity_data}

YOUR RESEARCH TASKS:

1. DEMAND VALIDATION (Find Pain Signals)

   Search {target_region} forums/platforms:
   {local_platforms}

   Search queries:
   - "{category} {native_language}"
   - "Alternative to {source_product}"
   - Pain keywords in native language

   Find 10-20 pain signals:
   - Forum posts asking for solutions
   - Complaints about existing tools
   - Questions about international products
   - Feature requests

   For each signal:
   - Source (platform name)
   - URL
   - Quote (in English)
   - Upvotes/engagement
   - Pain intensity (0-10)

2. MARKET SIZING

   Calculate TAM (Total Addressable Market):
   - How many companies in {target_region} could use this?
   - What's average spend per company on {category}?
   - TAM = # companies * avg spend

   Research:
   - Industry reports
   - Government statistics
   - Comparable markets

   Example calculation:
   - 15,000 construction companies in Poland
   - Average $3,000/year software spend
   - TAM = $45M

3. UNIT ECONOMICS CALCULATION

   Based on source product pricing: {source_pricing}
   Adjust for target market purchasing power

   Calculate:
   a) ACV (Annual Contract Value)
      - Recommended pricing for {target_region}
      - Usually 40-60% of US pricing

   b) CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
      - Digital marketing in {target_region}
      - Estimate: $500-1500 depending on ACV
      - Use CAC = ACV * 0.3 as baseline

   c) LTV (Lifetime Value)
      - Churn assumption: 20% annual (5-year avg lifetime)
      - Gross margin: 85% (SaaS standard)
      - LTV = (ACV * 0.85) / 0.20 = ACV * 4.25

   d) Metrics:
      - LTV:CAC ratio (should be >3:1)
      - Payback period (should be <18 months)
      - Magic Number (should be >0.75)

4. FINANCIAL MODEL (3-Year)

   Assumptions:
   - Start with 10 customers (design partners)
   - Growth: 300% Y1-Y2, 200% Y2-Y3
   - Gross margin: 85%
   - S&M spend: 40% of revenue (Y1), 30% (Y2), 25% (Y3)
   - R&D spend: 25% throughout
   - G&A spend: 15% throughout

   Calculate:
   Year 1: customers=30, revenue=$90k, EBITDA=-$30k
   Year 2: customers=120, revenue=$360k, EBITDA=$40k
   Year 3: customers=360, revenue=$1.1M, EBITDA=$250k

5. RISK ANALYSIS

   Identify top 3-5 risks:
   - Competitive (Could source product enter market?)
   - Economic (Is target region stable?)
   - Execution (Do we have right team?)
   - Regulatory (Any barriers?)

   For each risk:
   - Probability: low|medium|high
   - Impact: low|medium|high
   - Mitigation strategy

6. MOAT ANALYSIS

   Assess defensibility:
   - Network effects: 0-10
   - Switching costs: 0-10
   - Regulatory moat: 0-10
   - Brand/trust: 0-10

   Overall moat_score = average
   Window to build moat: X months
